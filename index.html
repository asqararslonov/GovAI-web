Capabilities

        
Designed for real government work

        
Everything below ships today. Where there's a caveat, we state it.

      

      
    
        
  1. 01
    Knowledge Base Shipped
    Point GovAI at a folder of decrees, codes or manuals. Chunking, storage and retrieval stay local in a single database file.
    2. 
        
  2. 02
    Copilots Shipped
    Role-specific assistants per department. The legal reviewer's copilot doesn't behave like procurement's.
    3. 
        
  3. 03
    Skills Shipped
    Save a prompt once as a /command. Every staff member gets the same phrasing and quality, every time.
    4. 
        
  4. 04
    Local models Not default
    Runs on Ollama, or MLX on Apple Silicon. Supported and tested, but signed-in users get a hosted gateway model unless they change it.
    5. 
        
  5. 05
    Model Context Protocol Shipped
    Connect Google Workspace, the filesystem or a document converter. Every connection is explicit and visible in settings.
    6. 
        
  6. 06
    Multilingual Shipped
    Uzbek, Russian and English are detected and answered in the language written, not translated afterwards.
    7. 
        
  7. 07
    Voice input & voice agent External
    Dictation and spoken conversation. Speech is routed to external services; no self-hosted production path yet.
    8.

How deployment works

        
From scoping to daily use

      

      

        
01
Scoping
Review documents, workflows and infrastructure. Agree in writing which external destinations are permitted.

        
02
Deployment
Install on the agency's own servers or workstations, connected to a local model or an approved provider.

        
03
Knowledge base
Ingest and index official documents, codes and procedures.

        
04
Onboarding
Configure Copilots and Skills per department. Train staff on what may and may not be entered.

        
05
Support
Updates, new integrations and wider document coverage as needs change.
